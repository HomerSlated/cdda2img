ECMA-130: Data interchange on read-only 120 mm optical data discs (CD-ROM)
2nd edition, June 1996

URL: https://www.ecma-international.org/wp-content/uploads/ECMA-130_2nd_edition_june_1996.pdf

Freely available from ECMA International at the above URL (no purchase required).

The document is primarily the CD-ROM data layer standard (Mode 1/Mode 2 sector format,
error correction, synchronisation, header, EDC/ECC), but it also contains a textual
representation of the Eight-to-Fourteen Modulation (EFM) lookup table (Annex A). This
is the same LUT used for CD-DA. The carrotIndustries/redbook decoder obtained the LUT
by OCR from IEC 60908; the textual form in ECMA-130 is an easier machine-readable
alternative.
